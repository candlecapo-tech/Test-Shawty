How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Each guess lights the lanterns to show how close you are.

  • A Gold: right letter, right spot
  • B Violet: in the word, wrong spot
  • C Dark: not in the word

Stuck? 💡 Hint reveals one letter, once per round. A new word every round, as many rounds as you like.
